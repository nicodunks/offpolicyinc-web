The strategy

The general, astra, wrote one strategy for the whole fight. The platoon leaders followed it.

"Damage dealers: concentrate on the enemy Archmage, then exposed Priests, other casters and ranged units. Finish priority targets together; switch when the target dies, disappears, becomes unsafe or a higher-priority support becomes exposed. Avoid grinding down the Mountain King while vulnerable supports remain. Healing and survival roles take precedence over following the damage dealers' target."

@BrainsAndTennis on X · 18 September 2026

How it ran

In this demo, astra acted as a general and came up with a strategy. 7 Individual Jevs acted as platoon leaders that followed this strategy, separately controlling heroes and unit groups. The micro obviously ain’t at the level of @followgrubby (my gaming goat), but as you can see, it executed on a strategy and made intelligent micro'ing decisions.

1000+ calls, or ~100 calls per unit group per minute, were made over the course of 2 minutes, which would have been too slow and expensive for most if not all llm apis. For jev it was about 20 cents.
